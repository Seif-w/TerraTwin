Analog Similarity Methodology

How TerraTwin scores terrestrial locations against extraterrestrial target zones.

1. Feature Extraction & Normalization

Raw planetary elevation (MOLA/LOLA) and Earth DEM rasters are converted into slope gradients, surface roughness, and thermal amplitude vectors normalized to [0, 1].

2. Multi-Factor Weighted Scoring Engine

FastAPI backend dynamically weights user-selected parameters (Topography, Geology, Temperature, Terrain, Resources, Environment) using cosine & Euclidean feature similarity.

3. Mission Readiness Ranking

Candidate sites are ranked by overall similarity percentage and operational accessibility for rover testing, EVA simulation, and ISRU (In-Situ Resource Utilization).

About TerraTwin

TerraTwin is an Earth-to-Moon & Mars terrestrial analog discovery platform built for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge. Before sending astronauts or rovers to the Moon or Mars, space agencies test hardware and habitats in Earth environments that mimic extraterrestrial geology and climate.

🇪🇬 Featured Case Study: Egypt's Western Desert

Our analysis highlights Egypt's Western Desert (White & Black Desert regions) as a premier Mars analog (97.1% similarity score), combining hyper-arid conditions, basaltic volcanic formations, and wind-eroded sedimentary terrains ideal for rover mobility and habitat testing.
